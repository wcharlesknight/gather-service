## Gather App 

Frontend Repository Location: /Users/charlieknight/Projects/LoopIn
Database: Firebase

## Concept

Gather app is meant to be a mobile application for people to get a weekly push notification of where to meet. They will sign up through the frontend, the project location is stated here Frontend Repository Location. It will start with just Seattle but will eventually expand to other cities. The locations are meant to be bars or any sort of hangout, we will send out the location on Thursday every week and people are meant to meet on Friday. The frontend mobile application will show where the last 4 meetups have been. 

## Future Expansion

Eventually we will offer an in app chat but this wont be until we get the notification service up and running.
